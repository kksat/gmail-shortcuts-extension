# Privacy Policy for Gmail Shortcuts

**Effective Date:** September 2026

**Gmail Shortcuts** ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the extension accesses and how it is handled.

---

### 1. Data Collection & Transmission
- **No Personal Data Collected:** The Extension does **not** collect, track, log, or transmit any personally identifiable information, email content, email addresses, recipient information, or browsing history.
- **No Remote Servers:** The Extension does not connect to any external or third-party servers. All scripts and operations execute locally within your browser.

---

### 2. Permissions & Data Storage
- **`https://mail.google.com/*`**: Required solely to inject the shortcut listener into Gmail so you can use keyboard shortcuts for snoozing and pagination. The extension does not read, parse, or store the contents of your emails.
- **`storage`**: Used exclusively through Chrome's native storage API (`chrome.storage.sync` / `chrome.storage.local`) to persist your local user preferences (custom shortcut keybindings, HUD timeout, and toggle settings).

---

### 3. Third-Party Sharing
The Extension does not sell, rent, share, or disclose any user information to third parties or advertising partners.
